Introduction

The rapid growth of e-commerce and digital retail has fundamentally transformed the way businesses operate and interact with their customers. In today's competitive market environment, even specialized product stores — such as those dealing in LED lighting — require a strong online presence to remain relevant and accessible to a wider audience. BrightWorld, a physical LED lighting retail store, identified this need and sought to develop a comprehensive web-based solution to showcase its products, attract customers, and provide detailed product information online.

The BrightWorld LED Lights Website is a fully responsive, multi-page static web application designed to serve as the official online storefront for BrightWorld. This website provides customers with a seamless digital experience, allowing them to browse through various categories of LED lighting products, explore product specifications and pricing, discover different brands, and make informed purchasing decisions — all from the comfort of their homes.

The website features a modern, premium design with a dark-mode interface, smooth animations, an interactive product filtering and search system, a shopping cart, customer feedback functionality, and downloadable product documentation. It is built entirely using front-end technologies — HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript — with Bootstrap 5 as the CSS framework.

This project not only fulfills the business requirement of establishing BrightWorld's online presence, but also demonstrates the practical application of modern web development skills, including responsive design, dynamic UI interactions, LocalStorage-based state management, and professional-grade visual design.

The website consists of 11 fully developed HTML pages, each serving a specific purpose — from product listings and brand showcasing to feedback forms, gallery displays, and downloadable resources. Every page is consistent in design, fully responsive across all screen sizes, and follows best practices in web development and user experience design.

Objectives of the Project

The primary objective of this E-Project is to design and develop a fully functional, visually appealing, and user-friendly Online LED Lighting Store Website for BrightWorld. This system aims to provide a centralized digital platform where customers can browse LED lighting products, access specifications and pricing, explore brand offerings, submit feedback, and download product documentation — all without visiting the physical store.

More specifically, the objectives of this project are:

  • To develop a professional, multi-page website that serves as BrightWorld's complete digital storefront, accessible to customers at any time from any device.
  • To display all LED product categories (Bulbs, Spot Lights, Decoration Lights, Smart Lights, Tube Lights, and Ceiling Lights) with images, specifications, and pricing.
  • To provide a simple, interactive, and fully responsive user interface that ensures ease of navigation for all types of users on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
  • To implement a real-time product search, category filter, and sorting system that helps customers quickly find the products they need.
  • To showcase multiple LED brands (Philips, Osram, Havells, Syska, Wipro, Bajaj, Orient, GM Modular) with dedicated brand pages and filtered product listings.
  • To include a customer feedback form that allows visitors to share ratings, reviews, and suggestions with the store management.
  • To provide downloadable product documentation — including Price Lists, Product Catalogues, Technical Specifications, and Warranty Guides — for customer convenience.
  • To implement a shopping cart system using LocalStorage that enables customers to add products, manage quantities, and review selections.
  • To apply a premium Dark Mode / Light Mode theme toggle with user preference persistence across sessions.
  • To apply modern web development tools and best practices learned during the course, including HTML5 semantics, CSS3 animations, JavaScript ES6+, Bootstrap 5, and AOS library.
  • To enhance teamwork, communication, and real-world problem-solving skills through collaborative project development within the team.

Problem Statement
Original Client Requirement (as provided): BrightWorld is having a store for LED Lights. They have a collection of lights. Now with the changing environment, they want their presence on the Internet. Hence they need to have a website with all their products listed on the website. Consumers should find it easy to view various products and decide upon purchasing the right product to meet their requirements. A website needs to be designed to display various lights available with the store. Details of locations, feedback must be included.

In the traditional retail model, customers of BrightWorld LED Lights are required to physically visit the store to browse products, check specifications, compare prices, and make purchasing decisions. This process is often time-consuming and inconvenient, especially for customers living far from the store, those with busy schedules, or individuals who prefer to research products before making a purchase.

The key problems addressed by this project are as follows:

  • Customers have no way to browse BrightWorld's complete product inventory online, leading to missed sales opportunities and reduced customer reach.
  • There is no digital platform where customers can compare product specifications, pricing, and brands without visiting the physical store.
  • The store has no mechanism for collecting customer feedback digitally, making it difficult to improve services based on customer opinions.
  • Product documentation such as price lists, technical specifications, and catalogues are not accessible to customers outside the store.
  • Customers cannot view the store's location, contact information, or operating hours without directly calling or visiting the store.
  • There is no organized way to showcase the multiple LED brands offered by the store, making it difficult for brand-conscious customers to identify their preferred products.
  • The lack of an online presence results in reduced visibility in an increasingly digital marketplace, where competitors with websites gain a significant advantage.

Due to these challenges, there is a strong need for a professional, user-friendly, and comprehensive website for BrightWorld. Such a platform will provide a centralized digital storefront where customers can browse products, view specifications and pricing, explore brand offerings, find store contact details, submit feedback, and download product resources — thereby improving customer experience and expanding the store's market reach.

Responsive image gallery with filter tabs (All, Interior, Exterior, Commercial, Home Decor), hover zoom/overlay effects, and lightbox for full-size image viewing.

Company history, mission & vision, team introduction, achievements, and reasons to choose BrightWorld.

6. Feedback Page — feedback.html

Customer feedback form: Name, Email, Phone, Product Category, Star Rating (1–5), Comments field, Submit button.

7. Contact Us — contact.html

Physical store address, map section, phone numbers, email, store hours (Mon–Sat: 9 AM – 10 PM), and direct contact form.

8. Queries & FAQ — queries.html

Accordion-style frequently asked questions and a query submission form for customer support.

9. Sitemap — sitemap.html

Visual HTML sitemap showing complete website page hierarchy for easy navigation reference.

10. Downloads — download.html

Four downloadable documents: Price List, Product Catalogue, Technical Specifications, and Warranty Guide.

Shopping cart with item display, quantity management, price calculation, and LocalStorage-based persistence.
