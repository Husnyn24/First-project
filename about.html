About BrightWorld

Bringing premium, energy-saving lighting solutions to Pakistan since 2009.

Introduction

Shining A Light On Our Journey

BrightWorld LED Lights Store started in 2009 with a simple vision: to replace inefficient conventional lights with highly durable, eco-friendly, and energy-saving LED solutions. Over the past 15 years, we have grown into one of the most trusted names in the electronics industry in Pakistan.

We are authorized retailers and project partners for top global brands, serving residential, commercial, and massive industrial projects with professional installation and unmatched support services.

Our Vision

To light up every household and corporate entity in Pakistan using the smartest, greenest, and most cost-effective LED technologies.

Our Mission

To provide authentic, top-grade certified lighting products with a seamless buyer experience, ensuring quality, trust, and exceptional service.

History

Our Growth Timeline

Take a quick walk down memory lane to see how we got to where we are today.

2009
BrightWorld Foundation

Inception as a small scale retailer in Hall Road, Lahore. Introduced energy savers and first-gen LEDs.

2013
Authorized Philips Partner

Signed official dealership agreement with Philips Lighting, launching premium quality LEDs in the local market.

2017
Gulberg Showroom Launch

Opened our state-of-the-art flagship experience store in Gulberg, Lahore, highlighting smart lighting and decor systems.

2021
Industrial Lighting Department

Launched a dedicated division for corporate and factory clients, securing over 100+ industrial lighting installations.

2025
Next-Gen Smart & Solar Integration

Broadened our catalog to smart IoT-enabled lighting controls and hybrid solar-powered street lighting options.
