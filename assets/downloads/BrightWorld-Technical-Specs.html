6. Installation Guidelines

  • Ensure power is switched off before installation
  • Verify supply voltage matches product rating (220–240V)
  • Do not exceed maximum wattage for fixtures
  • For outdoor products, ensure proper IP rating for weather exposure
  • Smart bulbs require 2.4GHz Wi-Fi – 5GHz not supported
  • Maintain minimum 10cm clearance around bulbs for heat dissipation
  • Contact BrightWorld for professional installation service
