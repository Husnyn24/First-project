What is Covered?

✔ Covered: Manufacturing defects, premature failure under normal use, significant lumen depreciation (below L70 in warranty period), electronic circuit failures, base/connector defects.

What is NOT Covered?

✘ Not Covered: Physical damage (cracking, breakage), water damage (for non-IP-rated products), damage from voltage fluctuations or surges, improper installation, use outside specified voltage range, normal wear and color shift in RGB products.

Warranty Terms & Conditions

  • Original purchase receipt / invoice must be presented for warranty claims
  • Warranty period begins from the date of purchase
  • Products must be installed by a qualified electrician
  • Products must be operated within specified voltage ranges (220–240V)
  • Replacement products carry warranty only for remaining original warranty period
  • BrightWorld reserves the right to inspect the product before approving any claim
  • In-store claims must be brought in original or protective packaging
  • For bulk orders, dedicated warranty support is provided through account managers
